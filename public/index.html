Hiding in
Drag 🎯 to move the play area. Drag 🫣 (or tap the map) to mark where it's hidden. Seekers never see 🫣.
The first ping waits at least 5 min after you mark the spot, so you can get away. Each ping alerts everyone, shrinks the hint circle around the hidden spot, and unlocks one hot/cold check. If nobody finds it one interval after the last ping, you win.

🔀 Move or rename

📡 Ping just fired. You get one move before the next ping.
Seekers get told it was renamed.
Drag 🫣 or tap the map to move it. It must stay inside the play area (dashed circle). Seekers only learn that it moved, not where. The next ping's hint circle restarts around the new spot, and moving it pushes the next ping to at least 5 min after you mark the new spot.
